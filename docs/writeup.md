# Write-up

For the case-study packet. Every figure is tagged.

## What was built

A collections path for loans 1 to 30 days overdue. Rules own the contact window, the attempt cap, identity-before-disclosure, required lines, the 25% first-payment floor, and the ban on charge waivers. The model owns intent, the promised date and amount, and a confidence score. A person owns hardship, disputes, complaints, distress, a human request, a below-floor plan, an already-paid claim the ledger does not support, a second identity failure, and confidence under 0.7.

Borrower-facing text is a template. The model draft is stored on the trace and is not concatenated onto the reply. A hostile draft that says the charges are waived does not reach the borrower. That is tested.

## What a reviewer should watch

The fourth demo fails the build without hurting the borrower. `python scripts/demo.py prompt-break` exits 1 because the fixture dropped a required line. The template the borrower hears still contains it. Those are different checks. Conflating them is how a prompt change ships.

The keyword pack, wrapped by the same policy, still cannot disclose a balance or confirm INR 500 a month. Intent can be wrong. The offer cannot. That is the Rifa line, implemented as a router rather than a prompt.
